    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 18 August 2026

    This Privacy Policy explains how PlainBetter handles personal information when you use plainbetter.co.uk. PlainBetter is currently an independent UK-based project in an early testing stage.

    For privacy or data protection requests, contact .

    1. Information we process

    Account information

    If you create an account, we process your email address, account identifier and authentication/session information. Authentication is provided by Supabase. PlainBetter does not receive or store your password in plain text.

    Language-learning information

    For logged-in users, we may store learning information such as your language, vocabulary lemmas, successful-use counts, successful Hardcore-test recalls and recorded chat-practice time.

    Conversation content

    When you use Chat or Generate Example Response, the text needed to provide the feature is sent to the PlainBetter backend and to OpenAI's API so a response can be generated. PlainBetter does not currently save full chat transcripts to your account database.

    Recent Conversations on your device

    If you choose to allow Recent Conversations, PlainBetter stores recent chat content in your browser's local storage. This is currently limited to 20 conversations and approximately 30 days. You can refuse or later withdraw this choice through Privacy choices in the footer.

    Technical and security information

    Our hosting, authentication, email and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, timestamps, request information, device/browser information and security logs as part of operating and protecting their services.

    2. Why we use this information

    We use personal information where it is needed to provide features you request, maintain your account and learning history, operate and secure the service, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse, and communicate with you about authentication or account matters.

    Where the law requires consent for optional storage on your device, such as the current Recent Conversations feature, we rely on your consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time.

    3. Browser storage

    PlainBetter uses essential browser storage for functions such as remembering authentication/session state when you choose to sign in and remembering your privacy choice. Optional Recent Conversations storage is disabled unless you allow it.

    PlainBetter does not currently use advertising or behavioural-tracking storage technologies on the site.

    4. Service providers

    We use third-party service providers to host and operate PlainBetter, manage user accounts and email, and provide AI-powered language features. Where necessary, these providers process personal information on our behalf.

    5. International processing

    Some service providers operate internationally, so personal information may be processed outside the UK. Where applicable, transfers are handled using safeguards and contractual arrangements made available by those providers and required by data-protection law.

    6. How long we keep information

    • Optional Recent Conversations stored on your device are limited to approximately 30 days and 20 conversations.
    • Account, vocabulary and learning-stat information is generally kept while your account remains active, unless you ask us to delete it or limited retention is reasonably required for legal, security or operational reasons.
    • Infrastructure and provider logs may have separate retention periods set by the relevant provider.

    7. Your rights

    Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, data portability and withdrawal of consent.

    To make a request, contact . You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office if you are unhappy with how your personal information is handled.

    8. Sensitive information

    Please avoid putting unnecessary sensitive personal information, confidential information, or personal information about other people into your chat messages or custom scenarios.

    9. Adults during the current beta

    PlainBetter is currently intended for adults aged 18 or over during this early testing stage.

    10. Changes to this policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy as PlainBetter develops, particularly if we add new storage, analytics, advertising, payment or account-history features.
